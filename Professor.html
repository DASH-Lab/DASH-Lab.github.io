Research Philosophy

Breaking Boundaries. My mission is to guide students beyond their comfort zones, fostering intellectual growth that transcends technical expertise. I encourage them to embrace diverse perspectives and challenge conventional thinking, cultivating both professional excellence and personal development.

Global Excellence. Our vision extends far beyond institutional or national recognition. I am committed to developing globally competitive scholars who contribute meaningfully to the international research community, establishing themselves as leaders in their fields.

Diversity as Strength. Having experienced two decades as an immigrant and minority scholar, I have witnessed firsthand the transformative power of diversity and mutual respect. I am dedicated to creating synergistic collaborations between Korean and international students, where different backgrounds and perspectives become catalysts for innovation and growth.
